On-Page SEO

What Basic On-Page Covers

These are the specific tasks I can deliver as part of an on-page SEO support role. I do not do technical SEO audits, site speed optimization, or schema markup without supervision from a senior SEO lead.

Heading Tag Structure

Review and rewrite H1, H2, and H3 tags for proper hierarchy, keyword placement, and readability.

Image Alt Text

Write descriptive, keyword-informed alt text for all images on target pages and blog posts.

Meta Titles & Descriptions

Write optimized meta titles (under 60 characters) and descriptions (150–160 characters) for target pages.

Internal Linking

Identify and suggest internal link opportunities from existing content to target pages using relevant anchor text.

Keyword Placement Review

Review existing content for natural keyword placement in titles, subheadings, and opening paragraphs.

Content Brief Writing

Write structured content briefs for writers including keyword targets, heading suggestions, and link placement instructions.
